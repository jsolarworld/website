## What these photos are

Almost all are **warehouse stock shots**: pallets and stacks of sealed boxes, plus close-ups of the shipping labels.
They prove what is in stock, and the labels give model and rating. They are **not** clean product photos and are not
suitable as the main image on a product page. The few unboxed shots (street lights, one solar panel) are the closest.
